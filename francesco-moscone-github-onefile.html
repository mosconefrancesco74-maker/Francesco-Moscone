About

Academic rigour. Public relevance.

Francesco Moscone is Professor of Business Economics at Brunel University London. His work spans health economics, prevention, healthcare sustainability, public finance and the wider economic effects of health and welfare policy.

He also teaches Public Economics at Ca’ Foscari University of Venice and is a Visiting Professor at Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore. His work has been discussed in international media and used in policy settings across the UK, Italy and European institutions.
